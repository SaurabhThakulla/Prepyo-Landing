EPS-TOPIK doesn't test literature or politics. It tests the Korean you need to live and work in Korea: reading signs, understanding instructions, talking about your health and your workplace. That makes the vocabulary predictable. Here are 60 words to learn first, grouped by topic.

## How to make the words stick

1. **Learn 10 words a day, not 60 at once.** Six days covers this whole list.
2. **Say every word aloud**, ideally while hearing a native recording. EPS-TOPIK has a listening section, so you need to recognise words by sound, not just on paper.
3. **Use spaced review.** Review new words the next day, three days later and a week later.
4. **Make example sentences.** *허리가 아파요* (my back hurts) sticks better than *허리* alone.
5. **Picture the workplace.** Imagine walking through a factory and naming what you see in Korean.

## Watch out for similar words

- **입구 / 출구**: entrance and exit. 입 means *enter*, 출 means *go out*. The same pair appears in 출근 (go to work) and 퇴근 (leave work).
- **오전 / 오후**: morning and afternoon. On notices and schedules, these tell you whether a time is a.m. or p.m.
