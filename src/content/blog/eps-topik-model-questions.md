Model questions are the fastest way to understand EPS-TOPIK, because the test reuses the same question **types** every time. Once you recognise a type, you know what it is testing and where to look.

Below is one sample question for each common type, with the answer and an explanation in English. **Prepyo wrote all of these questions.** They show the style of the test but are not real exam questions, which are not published.

## The test format

According to HRD Korea, which runs the test:

- **Reading:** 20 questions, 25 minutes, 50 points.
- **Listening:** 20 questions, 25 minutes, 50 points.
- **Total:** 40 multiple-choice questions, each with four options, in 50 minutes.

There is no speaking or writing section. Read more about [preparing for EPS-TOPIK without an academy](/blog/eps-topik-without-academy/).

## Reading model questions

### 1. Picture to word

*[Picture: a hammer]*

다음 그림을 보고 맞는 단어를 고르십시오.

① 망치 ② 가위 ③ 사다리 ④ 장갑

### 2. Correct underlined grammar

다음 중 밑줄 친 부분이 맞는 것을 고르십시오.

① 저는 사과<u>을</u> 먹어요.
② 친구<u>를</u> 만났어요.
③ 물<u>를</u> 마셔요.
④ 빵<u>를</u> 샀어요.

### 3. Signs and notices

*[A sign on a factory wall: **금연**]*

이 표지판은 무슨 뜻입니까?

① No smoking ② No entry ③ Wear a helmet ④ Emergency exit

### 4. Related word

다음 단어와 관계있는 것을 고르십시오. **병원**

① 의사 ② 요리사 ③ 운전기사 ④ 농부

### 5. Fill in the blank

저는 매일 아침 일곱 시에 회사( ) 갑니다.

① 를 ② 에 ③ 이 ④ 의

### 6. Word from description

다음 설명에 맞는 것을 고르십시오. **물건을 사고 돈을 내는 곳입니다.**

① 가게 ② 학교 ③ 공원 ④ 집

### 7. Matching statement

> 저는 김치 공장에서 일합니다. 아침 여덟 시에 일을 시작하고 오후 다섯 시에 끝납니다. 토요일과 일요일에는 쉽니다.

이 글의 내용과 같은 것을 고르십시오.

① 이 사람은 토요일에 일합니다.
② 이 사람은 오후 다섯 시에 일이 끝납니다.
③ 이 사람은 식당에서 일합니다.
④ 이 사람은 아침 아홉 시에 일을 시작합니다.

### 8. Workplace notice

> 내일은 비가 와서 밖에서 하는 일은 쉽니다. 안에서 하는 일은 평소처럼 합니다.

내일은 어떻게 됩니까?

① All work stops.
② Outdoor work stops.
③ Indoor work stops.
④ The factory closes early.

## Listening model questions

In the real test you hear these. Here the audio is written out in italics so you can check yourself. Better still, cover the script, have a friend read it aloud in Korean, and answer from listening alone.

### 9. Choose what you heard

*You hear: 가방*

① 가방 ② 가게 ③ 가수 ④ 가구

### 10. Choose the right reply

*You hear (man): 이 상자를 어디에 놓을까요?*

① 네, 감사합니다.
② 저기 창고에 놓으세요.
③ 상자가 아주 커요.
④ 어제 샀어요.

### 11. Numbers and prices

*You hear: 이 신발은 삼만 오천 원입니다.*

How much are the shoes?

① 3,500 won ② 35,000 won ③ 53,000 won ④ 30,500 won

### 12. What comes next

*You hear (woman): 실례합니다. 화장실이 어디에 있어요?*

What will the man most likely say next?

① 저쪽으로 쭉 가세요.
② 네, 맛있어요.
③ 다음 주에 와요.
④ 화장실을 청소했어요.

### 13. Dialogue comprehension

*You hear:*
*(woman) 민수 씨, 오늘 몇 시에 일이 끝나요?*
*(man) 여섯 시에 끝나요. 그런데 오늘은 한 시간 더 일해야 해요.*

What time does the man finish work today?

① 5:00 ② 6:00 ③ 7:00 ④ 8:00

## Answers and explanations

| No. | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ① 망치 | 망치 is a hammer. 가위 is scissors, 사다리 is a ladder and 장갑 is gloves, all common workplace words. |
| 2 | ② 친구를 | 친구 ends in a vowel, so it takes 를. 사과, 물 and 빵 need 를, 을 and 을 respectively. |
| 3 | ① No smoking | 금연 means "no smoking". Learn safety signs early, because they appear often. |
| 4 | ① 의사 | A hospital (병원) goes with a doctor (의사). |
| 5 | ② 에 | 에 marks the place you go to: 회사에 가요, "I go to the company". |
| 6 | ① 가게 | "A place where you buy things and pay money" is a shop (가게). |
| 7 | ② | The person finishes at 5 p.m. (오후 다섯 시). They rest on Saturdays, work in a kimchi factory and start at 8. |
| 8 | ② | 밖에서 하는 일은 쉽니다: outdoor work stops. Indoor work (안에서 하는 일) goes on as usual. |
| 9 | ① 가방 | The other options start with the same sound (가), which is exactly the trap this type sets. |
| 10 | ② | The question asks *where* to put the box. Only ② gives a place: "Put it in the storeroom over there." |
| 11 | ② | 삼만 오천 = 3 × 10,000 + 5 × 1,000 = 35,000. Practise 만 (10,000), because Nepali and English count differently. |
| 12 | ① | She asks where the toilet is. "Go straight that way" is the only answer to a *where* question. |
| 13 | ③ 7:00 | He normally finishes at six, but today he must work one hour more (한 시간 더), so seven. |

## How to use model questions well

1. **Learn the type, not the question.** Real questions change, but the types repeat. After each question, name the type and what it tested: a particle, a sign, a number or a place.
2. **Check every wrong option.** Ask why each wrong answer is wrong. That is where vocabulary sticks.
3. **Time yourself.** Each section allows about 75 seconds per question. Practise at that pace once your accuracy is steady.
4. **Listen to the end of every sentence.** In Korean, the ending decides the meaning: 들어가세요 (please go in) and 들어가지 마세요 (please don't go in) differ only at the end. See our [EPS-TOPIK listening guide](/blog/eps-topik-listening-tips/).
5. **Build vocabulary by topic.** Tools, safety and directions come up again and again. Start with our [60 workplace words](/blog/eps-topik-workplace-vocabulary/).
